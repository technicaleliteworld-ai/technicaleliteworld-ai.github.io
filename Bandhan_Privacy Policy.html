Privacy Policy for Bandhan

Effective Date: July 03, 2026

Welcome to Bandhan. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our Matrimony application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with the best matchmaking experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, gender, date of birth, religion, caste, education, occupation, income, and contact details (email and phone number).
  • Media and Photos: Photos you upload for your profile.
  • Location Data: We may collect your device's location to show nearby matches (only with your explicit permission).
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our app, including device information, IP address, and app usage statistics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your matrimony profile.
  • To provide accurate match recommendations based on your preferences.
  • To facilitate communication (chat and interest requests) between you and other users.
  • To improve the safety and security of our platform.
  • To send you important notifications and updates regarding your account.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your information only in the following cases:

  • With Other Users: Your public profile information (photos, bio, preferences) will be visible to other registered users to facilitate matchmaking.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party services (like Firebase, Razorpay) for authentication, database hosting, and payment processing.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, we may disclose your information to comply with legal obligations or protect our rights.

4. Data Security

We take data security seriously. We use industry-standard encryption and secure servers to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You have the right to delete your account and personal data at any time. You can request account deletion directly from the App settings or by contacting our support team. Once deleted, your public profile will be removed, and your data will be securely erased from our active databases.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
