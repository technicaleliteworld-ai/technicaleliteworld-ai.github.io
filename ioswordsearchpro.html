Privacy Policy

Apple App Store Compliant • Last Updated: October 10, 2026

WordGames ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting player privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our iOS application Word Search Game Word Find Pro handles user information, device data, and privacy permissions in strict compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1.

1. Data Collection & Usage

We believe in data minimization. We do not require users to register or supply personal identifiers such as legal names, physical addresses, or phone numbers to play the game.

  • Device & Gameplay Diagnostics: We collect non-personal technical data including iOS version, device model, app crash logs, level progress, puzzle scores, and time played. This data is utilized solely to maintain game state and optimize performance.
  • In-App Purchase Records: Handled natively by Apple StoreKit. We do not collect or store credit cards, bank accounts, or billing information.

2. App Tracking Transparency (ATT) & Advertising

In compliance with Apple's iOS App Tracking Transparency framework:

  • Our app displays the required iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt asking for your explicit consent before any advertising tracking takes place.
  • If you select "Ask App not to Track": The app will not access your Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). Ads served will be generic, non-personalized contextual ads only.
  • If you select "Allow": Third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) may utilize the IDFA to display ads relevant to your interests.
  • You can change this permission at any time in iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Word Search Game Word Find Pro.

3. Third-Party Partners & SDKs

Our app integrates trusted industry-standard SDKs that comply with Apple's privacy standards:

  • Google AdMob: For monetization and non-intrusive advertisements. (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: To monitor app performance, stability, and crashes. (Privacy Policy)
  • Meta Audience Network / Facebook SDK: For campaign attribution and ad delivery where permitted. (Privacy Policy)

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Apple Kids Guidelines)

Our game does not knowingly collect, request, or retain personal data from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that personal data of a minor under 13 was inadvertently received, we immediately delete it from our systems.

5. Data Retention

Locally saved game state (level unlock status, coins, settings) resides exclusively on your local iOS device storage. Cloud analytics data collected via Firebase is automatically deleted in accordance with standard retention schedules (typically 14 months).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I restore my previous in-app purchases?
A: Launch the game, open the in-game Settings menu, and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple will verify your Apple ID and unlock previously purchased non-consumable items without charging you again.
Q: The app crashes or freezes. What should I do?
A: Please make sure you have updated to the latest iOS version and app build. Force-closing the app from the iOS App Switcher and relaunching often resolves temporary memory glitches.
Q: How can I turn off background music or sound effects?
A: You can mute Sound FX or Music anytime by tapping the speaker or gear icon in the main game screen or pause menu.
Q: How do I manage personalized advertising?
A: You can toggle tracking permissions at any time via Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone or iPad.
